Roster shape

One "SLOT: N" per line, e.g. QB: 1, W/R/T: 1, BN: 6.

Weekly tuning (start/sit + waivers)

Each dial is a fraction of the decision at hand — the projection gap between the real options that week — not a point total. Defaults are the evidence-backed baseline: the only configuration in this project's history to hold up on a genuinely held-out season. ↺ restores one dial.

Draft tuning

Fractions of how much value is at stake in the current pick — the gap between the best available player and roughly the 25th best. Changes apply to an open draft room immediately; recorded picks are kept.
